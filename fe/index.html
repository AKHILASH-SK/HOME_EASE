Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our home service appointment system.

❓ How are your technicians verified?

Every technician undergoes a 3-tier screening process: police verification, trade certificate licensing check, and a hands-on technical diagnostic assessment before being inducted.

❓ Do I need to pay in advance?

No. HomeEase does not charge advance fees for standard appointments. You inspect the completed repair and pay the technician directly via Cash or UPI on your doorstep.

❓ What if the problem happens again?

All completed repairs are covered under our 30-Day Free Rework Guarantee. If the same issue reoccurs, our technician revisits and rectifies it without any visit charges.

❓ Which cities and zones do you cover?

We currently offer complete coverage across Coimbatore (RS Puram, Peelamedu, Gandhipuram, Saravanampatti, etc.), Chennai, and Bangalore.
